# Disclaimer

RVAI is an independent, open-source project. It helps people find free and legal ways to do what they would otherwise need paid software or games for.

## What RVAI does not do

RVAI will not help you:

- download, share or use cracked, pirated or "free full version" copies of paid software or games;
- find or generate serial numbers, license keys or keygens;
- bypass DRM, license checks, trial limits, paywalls or anti-cheat systems;
- decompile, disassemble or reverse engineer paid software;
- rip or copy art, music, models, levels, fonts or other assets out of a paid product;
- build a copy that uses another product's name, logo, characters or look.

Requests like these are refused, and RVAI offers legal options instead.

## Accuracy

License names, features, platforms, links and discounts come from a hand-maintained catalog and can go out of date. Always check the project's official website and its license before you install, use or redistribute it. "Free" in RVAI means free of charge; entries that are free but not open source are labeled as such.
