# n8n Reference Brief: RVAI About and navigation

- **Reference:** https://n8n.io/. It was supplied by the user as a visual and product reference, not a template.
- **Verification:** this session had no browser or web access. The patterns below come from general familiarity with n8n's public site, not a fresh capture. Re-check them against the live page before relying on specifics.

## Patterns observed (high level)
1. **One-sentence proposition first.** A short headline says what the product does and for whom. A single subline adds the "how".
2. **Compact top navigation.** A few product links, then a low-emphasis secondary action next to one high-contrast primary CTA.
3. **A strong, repeated CTA.** The same primary action appears in the header, the hero and later sections.
4. **Readable workflow language.** The product is explained as connected steps ("nodes"), and the diagram itself teaches the concept.
5. **Visible community and open-source framing.** Source availability and community are presented as a reason to trust the product, often in a contrasting band.
6. **Calm, roomy sections.** Each section makes one point, with one heading and one action.

## What RVAI adapts
- **Proposition:** "Find it. Extend it. Or create it yourself." The subline names RVAI's audience (students who can't afford expensive software or powerful computers) and its three paths.
- **Navigation:** the four tabs stay (About / Alternatives / Innovate / RVAI OpenSource). The header has a quiet "Sign up" button and a navy primary button, "Try Innovate".
- **Try first:** the hero's primary CTA and the "Try first" card enter Innovate in preview-demo mode with no sign-up step. The header keeps "Preview demo · no account" visible. Alternatives stays secondary: a text link plus the second choice in the service chooser.
- **Workflow:** a four-node strip (Describe → Find → Extend → Create) built from CSS and HTML only. A three-node strip on RVAI OpenSource shows Build → Publish (later) → Reuse (later).
- **Community band:** a navy section, "Built by students, open to students", points to RVAI OpenSource. It states plainly that the library is empty until publishing launches.
- **Palette:** existing blue/teal UI tokens plus the provisional navy/petrol/cyan/aqua tokens from `docs/palette-reference.md`. No water texture, and no red as a primary action color.

## What RVAI deliberately does not copy
- No n8n text, logos, illustrations, screenshots, icons, fonts, colors, or node-canvas visuals.
- No social proof: no user counts, star counts, company logos, testimonials or "trusted by" rows. RVAI has no verified figures, and the evidence slots stay "Team research/source pending".
- No pricing, enterprise or integrations-catalog sections. They don't fit RVAI's mission or current scope.
- No implied capability. The workflow nodes describe the conversation paths that exist in the scripted demo. Real AI generation, accounts and publishing remain Anna's backend work and are labeled "not connected" or "later".
