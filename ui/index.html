THE PROBLEM

Good tools sit behind paywalls

Professional design and building software is often expensive, tied to one platform, or too heavy for the devices many students have. Free options exist, but they’re scattered and hard to judge.

OUR MISSION

Possibility, without the paywall

Give every student a path from an idea to a working tool, without piracy and without starting from zero. What one student builds should help the next.

COMMUNITY LIBRARY · COMING LATER

RVAI OpenSource

An open library of original projects students build with RVAI, free to reuse, remix and learn from. Every project starts as one student’s idea and becomes a starting point for the next.

Not the same as Alternatives. Alternatives lists existing third-party software made by other communities. RVAI OpenSource will hold original projects created with RVAI and published by their makers.

No published projects yet

Publishing isn’t available in this prototype, so the library is empty. Nothing is listed here, real or sample. The first projects will come from students using Innovate.
